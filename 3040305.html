Perform 1 · Required

Build a title slide in Google Slides

Use your teacher-assigned storybook Slides file. If none is assigned, start a blank Slides presentation; do not change another student's work.

  1. Create your text boxes. Choose Insert → Text box; click and drag on the slide. Make one box for Story Title, one for Author, and one for Publisher.
    • Click the edge of a box to move the whole object; double-click inside to edit words.
    • If you make a mistake, use Undo (Ctrl+Z) or select the box edge and press Delete.
  2. Replace placeholder words. Type your story title, your author name, and a publisher name into the three separate boxes.
  3. Create clear hierarchy. Select the title text and change its font size and boldness in the toolbar. Make author and publisher smaller but readable.
  4. Arrange the page. Drag the boxes to make a clear reading order. Use alignment guides as you move them; leave comfortable space between the boxes.
  5. Check and protect. Read the slide as a younger reader. Fix overlaps, then select Slide → Duplicate slide as a safe copy before experimenting further. Keep the version you intend to share.
Done when: one title slide has three separate readable text boxes and at least two clear levels of text emphasis.
