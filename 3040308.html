Warm-Up

Name one design choice, one file-management habit, and one revision skill you gained during this book project.

Learn Together · BenQ

Look at the teacher's page examples and discuss what a young reader would notice.

  1. Review what the published reader sees: readable text, correct page order, meaningful illustrations, title and author, and consistent layout.
  2. Recall how to select publication tools, avoid or fix text-image overlaps, recover and organize source files, and verify exported PDF pages.
  3. Today's assessment asks you to apply the same TEKS to new scenarios; answer with exact tools and reasons rather than vague praise.

Whole-class checkpoint: Explain which change would help a reader and why before moving to computers.

Practice Together · Teacher demonstration

  1. On the BenQ display a mock book page with a crowded title, picture overlapping the story and tiny body text. Students identify three fixes aloud.
  2. Model how to justify each repair using a specific control such as font size, text box positioning or image order.
  3. Review the difference between an editable Slides source and a distributable PDF, then release students to individual assessment questions.

Try it aloud: Name the exact tool and the reader benefit of one modeled improvement.

Perform · Work on your actual storybook

  1. Complete all four independent TEKS mastery questions, giving exact tools, examples and explanations; submit each through its matching school Form.
  2. Open the finished Slides book and confirm the entire story is present in reading order, with readable pages, deliberate illustrations, and a clear cover.
  3. Export the final PDF and check each page. Retain the editable Google Slides source.
  4. Submit the finished file or link using the assignment route given in Google Classroom or by the teacher; do not create a new public-sharing setting without directions.
  5. Confirm the submitted version is correct and record any remaining recovery action before the unit closes.

Definition of finished: A readable complete book, intentional illustrations, editable source file and checked PDF when exporting is assigned. Do not sacrifice legibility for perfect art placement.
