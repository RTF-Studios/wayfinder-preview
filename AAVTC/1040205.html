06 · Explain your thinking

Mastery Question 2

A local nature center needs an ORIGINAL illustrated guide sign for young visitors. Scene A uses realistic textured trees, narrow paths, tiny labels and muted contrast. Scene B uses a simplified tree silhouette, clear path direction, large labels and bold contrast. Compare at least TWO formal design qualities and the likely intent of EACH scene. Recommend either design for the nature center's small printed sign, explaining how its educational context and audience affect your judgment. A well-supported choice of either can earn full credit.
