03 · Try it together

Group Practice

Together, treat the two examples as advertisements for the same watch and audience. Identify which formally communicates the message more clearly and propose a measurable improvement.

  1. Read the weak sample from normal viewing distance and say what is hard to recognize.
  2. Use the improved sample to point out contrast, a focal point and enough space for the action.
  3. Practice giving feedback as feature → effect → precise improvement, rather than simply saying “better”.

05 · Apply it

Perform

Revise your existing bandwagon billboard using viewer feedback and a purposeful before-and-after comparison.

  1. Open your existing Photopea PSD and keep the 2000 × 1000 canvas.
  2. Ask a partner to look for a few seconds and name the product, persuasive message and action.
  3. Record two observations and identify the largest communication problem.
  4. Make one specific improvement in contrast, size, spacing, wording or image position.
  5. Check spelling, information source, message, audience/receiver and bandwagon persuasion. Save and reopen your PSD.
