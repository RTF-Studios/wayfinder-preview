Learning Target

Warm-Up

What should a viewer remember after seeing your billboard for three seconds?

Learn · Specific feedback

Good critique identifies a design feature, its effect, and a practical improvement. Check the product, bandwagon persuasion, and intended viewer.

Perform · Revise your design

  1. Open your existing Photopea PSD. Keep the 2000 × 1000 canvas.
  2. Ask a partner to inspect it briefly and name the product, persuasive message, and action.
  3. Record two observations. Identify the most important communication problem.
  4. Make a specific improvement in contrast, sizing, spacing, wording, or image position.
  5. Check spelling, source, message, audience/receiver, and bandwagon technique. Save and reopen your PSD.

iCEV rubric: Research & Organization 35; Concept & Understanding 35; Creativity/Craftsmanship 15; Production/Effort 15.

Compare before and after

Before: A billboard has faint, tiny words and a crowded product image. A viewer cannot identify its action.

After: A large high-contrast bandwagon headline sits above one clear product picture and a concise action.

Which design serves the target audience better? Name two precise reasons.

Mastery Question ·

Compare two sample designs for the same audience. Evaluate their formal qualities, infer intended messages, and justify which better fulfills the purpose.

Example A: crowded layout with small pale text. Example B: one large headline, a clear product image and wide spacing. Both advertise the same watch to teenagers.
