Open-notes recap · Previously taught material

Recall Your Vector Skills

Use this short reference or your own class notes. The notes explain vocabulary, not the solutions to the four assessment scenarios.

Primitive: basic editable rectangle, square, circle or oval. Aspect ratio: width compared with height. Silhouette: outer contour of a character. Grouping: moving separate editable pieces together. Layer order: which overlapping object appears in front. Reshape anchor: one editable point on a vector path, not a bounding-box handle. Extended primitive: a simple derived shape with small deliberate changes. Shape language: meaningful geometric proportions. Negative space: gaps around and between shapes. Volume: apparent thickness/depth in a flat image.

Mascot: an original recognizable character representing an event, team or cause. Visual hierarchy: what an audience notices first. Iteration: improving a design after checking whether it communicates clearly.

After the Major · About 25 minutes

Perform · Original Thanksgiving/Harvest Mascot

Client brief: Create one original friendly character to represent a school Community Harvest Celebration. Use the shape control, extended primitive and professional-design thinking practiced this week.

  1. Choose your original mascot idea · Click for help
    Create an original friendly mascot for an imaginary Community Harvest Celebration attended by middle-school families. Consider a turkey, pumpkin, leaf, woodland creature or a harvest robot.
  2. Build a readable figure · Click to expand extra help
    In locally installed TurboWarp Vector Paint, assemble a roughly six-to-twelve-piece head/body/limb design using basic ovals/rectangles. Make the silhouette clear before adding color.
  3. Apply extended primitives sparingly · Click to expand extra help
    Use a few simple altered primitives if they improve your mascot. Move only a small number of anchors; no tracing, animation, scripts, complex background or logo.
  4. Group, layer and iterate once · Click to expand extra help
    Check one intentional negative-space gap and a front/back arrangement; group related parts. View your mascot small and make one meaningful geometry adjustment.
  5. Save and attach · Click to expand extra help
    Save the editable native TurboWarp project and attach the file to the Google Classroom assignment. Check the attachment name, not only the screenshot.

Finish line: recognizably original mascot; editable grouped geometric pieces; one intentional gap and layer choice; native project file attached in Google Classroom. This is a short final design, not a full scene.
