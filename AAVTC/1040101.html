02 · Discover a new art form

Vector art: the artwork that can grow

Could you design one mascot that appears on a tiny phone icon, a T-shirt, and the side of a school bus—without redrawing it three times? That is one reason professional artists use vector graphics.

Simple definition: Raster art is built from many tiny colored squares called pixels. Vector art is built from editable points, lines, shapes and curves. A raster image remembers colored pixels; a vector file remembers the geometry of the design.

Aliasing: why some curves look like stairs

Aliasing is the jagged staircase that appears when a curve or diagonal is shown on a limited pixel grid. Antialiasing blends some edge pixels into intermediate colors so the edge looks smoother. Antialiasing makes edges look better; it cannot magically recover lost details in a small raster photo. Even vector graphics are antialiased when drawn onto pixel-based screens. Their advantage is that the underlying shapes can be recalculated for each size.

How do professional artists choose?

Vector is strong for…Raster is strong for…
Logos, road signs, SVG app icons, crisp cartoons, diagrams, stickers, scalable bannersPhotography, realistic illustration, clouds, skin, complex textures, digital painting
Changing individual curves, colors, fills, shapes and outlinesPainting or editing individual pixels, brush strokes, photo details and texture
A clean original image at many sizes, if the design stays readableRich photographic detail at its original resolution

Real-world example: Google's Material Symbols icons are offered as scalable SVG paths. A cartoon mascot may use vector shapes because artists can edit its eyes, ears and silhouette independently. Professional artists also mix both techniques: a vector logo can sit on top of a raster photograph.

What about Pixar-style 3D animated movies?

History → future

Before digital design, artists painted signs, drafted curves, cut shapes and prepared printing plates by hand. Computers brought editable paths and Bézier curves; SVG later helped scalable graphics work on the web. Today vectors appear in branding, illustration, motion graphics and game interfaces.

Tomorrow, AI may help produce ideas and draft shapes, but an artist still chooses what communicates well, checks originality and permissions, and fixes weak proportions or confusing designs.

Discuss: Is a photo automatically worse than a vector drawing? No—different jobs need different tools. Which image would you choose for an eagle wildlife photograph? Which for an eagle-shaped school logo?

Visual design matters: Scalable does not mean readable. A tiny icon still needs bold shapes, contrast, good proportions and negative space.

03 · Think like a designer

Vector and raster: what's different, what's shared?

Both can communicate a story, use color, be displayed on screens, and be printed. They differ in how the source image is constructed and edited. Compare the diagrams, then fill in this Venn diagram independently or with a partner.

Talk it through: A 32-pixel icon looks blurry when stretched to a huge poster. Why? Would antialiasing restore the missing detail? Why might the original artist prefer an editable vector file?

Words to remember: vector, raster, pixel, path, Bézier curve, aliasing, antialiasing, scalable, silhouette, rendering.
