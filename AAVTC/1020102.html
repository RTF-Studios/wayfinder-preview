05 · Apply it

Perform

Continue the same bandwagon billboard from Day 1. Keep your original product, audience and communication plan.

  1. Open your existing Photopea PSD. Confirm that the canvas is still 2000 × 1000 pixels and the product is the one in your plan.
  2. Create a very short, large bandwagon headline. Add the product name and a separate action line; ensure the wording is accurate.
  3. Add a product image you own, drew, or are allowed to use. Move and transform it into a single clear focal point.
  4. Improve contrast, align important objects, and use white space instead of filling every corner.
  5. Zoom out until the whole sign fits your screen. Check product, bandwagon message and action; save the editable PSD.
