06 · Check claims before Question 2

Independent Mini Learn

Checking a claim means looking for evidence rather than guessing. Examine the source, independent tests, and whether a claim applies to all audiences. Different checks answer different questions.

Claim: “Works for everyone.”

Check 1: Can a reliable source support the claim?

Check 2: Was it tested for different people or situations?

Neither check guarantees universal results. A responsible advertisement avoids promises it cannot demonstrate.

Name one source you could consult and one different kind of evidence you would inspect before trusting a product claim.

Creative Labs

Creative Lab 1

Headline test

1. Draft two truthful bandwagon headlines for your selected product. 2. Compare tone and readability. 3. Choose the stronger one and explain why.

Example: “Join the adventure” and “Discover your next adventure.” Both invite an audience without inventing statistics.

Optional starters: “I tried…” / “I chose this because…” / “The reader would notice…”

Creative Lab 2

Audience lens

1. Choose two realistic audiences for the same product. 2. Explain how the billboard message would change. 3. Select one audience and justify the wording.

Example: A family audience needs different language than a student audience, even for the same product.

Optional starters: “I tried…” / “I chose this because…” / “The reader would notice…”

Creative Lab 3

Visual map

1. Sketch or describe the first, second and third elements viewers notice. 2. Explain why you placed them that way. 3. Check that no important words are crowded.

Example: Headline first → product image second → action last.

Optional starters: “I tried…” / “I chose this because…” / “The reader would notice…”
