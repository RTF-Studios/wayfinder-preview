Original story · supplied reading resource

Moss and the Rumor

Use the author's original words, in their original order. You are the publisher, not the author.

At the edge of the foggy playground lived Moss, a shy green swamp monster. More than anything, Moss wanted someone to play with. One morning, a crow whispered, “Moss scares anyone who comes near.” Soon the other creatures moved away whenever Moss walked past. Moss sat alone beside the empty swings. Then a tiny hatchling wandered into the fog and could not find its family. Everyone heard its frightened chirps, but the mist hid the path. Moss knew the swamp trails by heart. “Follow my footsteps,” Moss said gently. Step by step, Moss led the hatchling home. The other creatures gathered around. “We believed a rumor instead of getting to know you,” one apologized. “Would you like to play?” Moss smiled. The next morning, the swings were no longer empty.

  2. Check title, credit, body-text readability, logical pages and illustrations. Use the original author credit where given; do not claim authorship of supplied writing.

Celebration · speaking and listening

Random-Wheel Project Showcase

Everyone prepares. The teacher randomly selects a flexible number of student presenters, usually about three. Be ready to share what you made, one design decision you are proud of, and what you might change next.

  1. Presenters: show your storybook and speak for about two minutes. Explain your reader, your layout choices and a point of pride.
  2. Audience: observe carefully and offer Two Stars (two specific strengths) and a Wish (one useful possible improvement).
  3. Everyone records something they noticed and can apply to their own future publications. Be respectful and specific; never rank classmates by popularity.

Presenter rotation is managed privately by the teacher; selection history is not shown here.

If you finish early

Optional independent challenge: make an alternative final-page layout using exactly the same story words; explain how it would serve your audience better. Do not start a new app or replace assessment and showcase responsibilities.
