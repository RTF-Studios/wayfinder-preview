Warm-Up

A driver sees your sign for only a few seconds. Which words should be readable first?

Learn · A quick design insight

Strong hierarchy uses size, contrast, placement, and space to guide the eye. Limit the amount of text; a billboard is not an essay.

iCEV source: Communication Styles — Billboard Design. This project must use bandwagon persuasion and identify its information source, message, and audience/receiver.

Perform · Build the billboard

  1. Step 1. Open your saved PSD in Photopea. Confirm the canvas is still 2000 × 1000 px and the intended product matches your project plan.
  2. Step 2. Create a very short, large bandwagon headline. Add the product name and a separate action line. Check that the writing is accurate and easy to understand.
  3. Step 3. Add a product picture you own, drew, or can legally use, or create an original illustration. Use the move/transform controls to establish a clear focal point.
  4. Step 4. Give the headline and product strong contrast against the background. Align major items; use white space rather than filling every corner.
  5. Step 5. Zoom out until the whole billboard fits comfortably on screen. Check whether the product, bandwagon message, and action remain immediately recognizable. Save your PSD.

Production note: A strong billboard communicates at a glance. Prioritize readable words, an obvious visual focal point, and a specific audience over decoration.

iCEV rubric: Research & Organization 35; Concept & Understanding 35; Creativity/Craftsmanship 15; Production/Effort 15 (100 total).

Creative Labs · Three required challenges

All three are graded evidence tasks. Use only skills already practiced for this billboard.

Creative Lab 1 · Contrast trial

Try a second color pairing in your working file and explain which provides clearer readability.

Creative Lab 2 · Composition revision

Make one intentional change to balance or spacing, record before/after observations, and state the effect.

Creative Lab 3 · Small-view test

View the billboard at a much smaller scale and identify which element survives, which fails, and the correction you made.
