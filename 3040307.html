Proofread, Export and Test the Book

Day 7 · 3040307

Learning Target

I can proofread and export a book that is readable on every page.

Priority TEKS: §126.18(c)(12)(H) — finalize and verify a digital publication

Secondary: §126.18(c)(12)(B), §126.18(c)(12)(C), §126.18(c)(11)

Warm-Up

Why is a PDF preview still necessary after a book looks good in Google Slides?

Learn Together · BenQ

Look at the teacher's page examples and discuss what a young reader would notice.

  1. Proofread twice: once for story meaning/spelling, and once for visual layout. Read page by page as a younger reader would.
  2. Your editable Google Slides file is the working source. A PDF is a stable reading copy. Export does not replace saving the original.
  3. The final PDF can reveal cut-off words, odd margins, missing illustrations or unexpected reading order. Always open the PDF before submitting.

Whole-class checkpoint: Explain which change would help a reader and why before moving to computers.

Practice Together · Teacher demonstration

  1. Show a sample page with three problems: overlong text, an image covering a sentence, and a misspelled title. Ask students to identify and prioritize fixes.
  2. Model one edit in Slides, export or download a PDF, then open and inspect the exported page.
  3. Ask students to explain what they would check if a shared PDF were missing or unreadable.

Try it aloud: Name the exact tool and the reader benefit of one modeled improvement.

Perform · Work on your actual storybook

  1. Read the full book carefully. Correct spelling, punctuation, unfinished sentences and missing passages without changing the original story unnecessarily.
  2. Review every page at its normal reading size; resolve clipped text, images covering sentences, inconsistent fonts and accidental blank pages.
  3. Verify book title, author information and page order. Save your editable Slides project.
  4. Use File → Download → PDF document in Google Slides. Open the downloaded PDF and inspect each page, especially illustrated pages.
  5. If the PDF has errors, repair the Slides source, export again and recheck. Keep both source and PDF ready for submission.

Definition of finished: A readable complete book, intentional illustrations, editable source file and checked PDF when exporting is assigned. Do not sacrifice legibility for perfect art placement.
