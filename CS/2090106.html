Connect the learning · 4 minutes

How the Pieces Fit Together

A web developer begins with HTML to organize content and label elements. CSS controls their visual presentation, including readable colors, spacing and typography. Flexbox helps arrange content panels. Responsive rules adapt those arrangements to different viewport widths. Finally, a developer checks that the result is understandable to people and meets exact automated requirements. Each section of this test follows part of that workflow.

This connection is your study map, not an answer key. You will need to apply what you learned to new examples.
