Warm-up · 5 minutes

Two covers. One story.

Both covers contain exactly the same words, but a young reader will notice them in a different order. On a book cover, the title should be easy to find first.

Look carefully: Size, weight and placement tell the eye where to begin. Now answer Question 1.

Practice · 1–2 minutes

Move a text box

  1. In your attached Slides file, click Insert → Text box. Draw a box and type My Story.
  2. Click outside the box. Click the border, not the letters.
  3. Drag the box up. Click Undo to move it back.

Keep this same file open. You will change My Story to your real title.

Perform 1 · Required creation

Make your book-cover slide

  1. Your title: Replace My Story with your real title. Make it large (about 36–44 pt).
  2. Author: Insert a second text box. Type your author credit below the title, smaller (about 18–24 pt).
  3. Position: Drag the box borders to line up the title and author. Leave enough space to read both.
  4. Check: Can someone find the title first? Fix anything hard to read. Slides saves automatically.

Keep the file attached to Classroom. Do not turn it in until your teacher tells you to.

Find the title at a glance

  1. Duplicate your title slide.
  2. Try a different title size.

B · Two Moods

Same words, different feeling.

  1. Duplicate your slide; keep the words unchanged.
  2. Make a calm, evenly spaced version.
  3. Make a dramatic version using size or boldness.
  4. Check that both remain readable.
  5. Describe how the mood changed.
