One title slide. Real Google Slides skills. Design that helps readers find the story.

★ Learning target

We will create and format text boxes in Google Slides so that a book’s title and author are easy for our intended readers to recognize.

Warm-up · 5 minutes

Two covers. One story.

Both covers contain exactly the same words, but a young reader will notice them in a different order. On a book cover, the title should be easy to find first.

Look carefully: Size, weight and placement tell the eye where to begin. Now answer Question 1.

Tiny practice · 1–2 minutes

Move the box, not the words

  1. Make a temporary text box containing My Story.
  2. Click outside it. Then click the box border.
  3. Drag it higher. Press Undo to return it.

Done? You can tell when the whole box, rather than its text, is selected.

Perform · Required creation

Make your own title slide

Build one readable, editable cover slide—not the entire storybook.

  1. Open your presentation.Use the approved class deck. If none was provided, open a blank Google Slides file under your school account. Follow the class naming directions.
  2. Add the story title.Insert → Text box → drag across the upper area → type your original title. Select the words and try a large readable size (about 36–44 pt).
  3. Add the author credit.Create a separate text box. Type the appropriate author name or credit. Use a smaller size (about 18–24 pt). Add a publisher only if your project requires one.
  4. Line up the boxes.Click the boxes' borders to move them. Keep the title strongest, author easy to read, and generous empty space between.
  5. Review and save.View the slide at normal size. Use Undo/Delete if needed. Keep the editable copy in your school account; follow your teacher's actual submission directions.

Creative Labs · Optional · Ungraded

Three ways to experiment

Finish your required work first. Duplicate your slide before experimenting so your original stays safe. Choose any Lab; there is no requirement to complete all three.

A · Title Tower

Make the same title easy to find in a new way.

  1. Duplicate your title slide.
  2. Try a different title size.
  3. Move the title to another readable location.
  4. Ask someone which version is clearer.
  5. Choose your strongest version and explain why.

B · Two Moods

Same words, different feeling.

  1. Duplicate your slide; keep the words unchanged.
  2. Make a calm, evenly spaced version.
  3. Make a dramatic version using size or boldness.
  4. Check that both remain readable.
  5. Describe how the mood changed.

C · Alignment Detective

Repair a cover that's awkward to read.

  1. Duplicate the slide.
  2. Move the author into an awkward position.
  3. Notice why it feels harder to scan.
  4. Fix it with border dragging and guides.
  5. Show the before and after.
